## Phase 1 decisions (3 Jul 2026)

- **PDF route decided by spike** (session scratchpad, `spike/spike.py`): HTML/CSS per-supplier templates → headless Chrome `--print-to-pdf` → **pypdf normalisation** (pages rebuilt, fixed metadata, zeroed /ID, XMP stripped). Raw Chrome output differs per run; normalised output is **byte-identical across runs** (SHA-256, verified). pdfplumber recovers the full text layer with coordinates, Finnish characters intact. Versions pinned in `format_research.md` §8 (Chrome 149.0.7827.201, pypdf 5.8.0, pdfplumber 0.11.10); byte-identity claims are relative to the pinned Chrome — the HTML and answer key are deterministic unconditionally. **Deviation from spec recorded**: weasyprint (spec's suggestion) rejected — not installed, GTK dependency on Windows; reportlab `invariant` remains the tested-available fallback.
- **In-world window: calendar 2025.** The Finnish 14% VAT class becomes 13.5% on 1 Jan 2026 (vero.fi, found in research); a 2025 window keeps rates stable at 25.5/14/10 and matches the project spec's rates. The window also predates ViDA's 2030 structured-e-invoicing mandate, which the writeup can cite as the reason the PDF problem is a today-problem.
- **The e-invoicing defence is designed in, not bolted on**: Pyökkipaja's corpus is explicitly the PDF *residue* of an otherwise Finvoice-run AP (~85% of Finnish B2B invoices were structured by 2022; cross-border and small suppliers are the documented PDF holdouts — sources in `format_research.md` §3). Design.md §1–§2 carry this framing; Eva open question 3.
- **Fictional company: Pyökkipaja Oy** (Lahti contract-furniture maker, ~120 staff). Scout collision check 3 Jul 2026: Pyökkipaja clear; Vaahterakaluste clear (fallback); Lehmuskaluste clear; **Visakoivu Kaluste rejected** (Visu Kaluste Oy exists — laboratory furniture, Visuvesi). Lahti furniture-cluster setting verified (ISKU, 2,700+ regional employment). Checked against the other cases' fictional worlds (Kataja/Storsund/Dashlume, Jalavakoski, Paju + customer roster) — no overlaps. **Supplier names in design §3 are provisional**; a dedicated scout collision check runs before Phase 2 build (Case 5 precedent: its customer-name check caught four real companies).

1. Buyer block overlapping the line-table header (s3/s4/s5 — text collision).

4. VAT rates printed with a decimal point ("25.5") on fi/de/sv documents — locale demands "25,5"; formatting.format_rate made locale-aware (shared with the validator's re-render checks).

7. **Literal "None" rendered on the s3 credit note ("Zahlungsbedingungen: None")** — the worst tell in the corpus; nullable fields now render as absence, and rule 2 gains a no-"None"-token sub-check so this class cannot regress.
8. s7 canteen semantics ("3 ateria à 162,37" for a monthly staff canteen) → realistic meal counts at per-meal prices.
9. Hotel one-off incoherence (description "2 vrk", quantity 1) → quantity 2 × per-night price.

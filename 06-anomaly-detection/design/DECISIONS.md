## 3 July 2026 — Phase 3 detection run: THE FROZEN DEVIATION (the detector misses its designed catches)

- Detection layer built (python-builder; `run_rule_layer.py`, `detect_anomalies.py`, `prepare_clusters.py` — no answer-key access anywhere on the detection path, grep-verified; `detect_anomalies.py` read line-by-line in the main loop; determinism asserted in-process and across processes, sklearn 1.9.0). Rule layer reproduced 14/18/4/10/10/8/0 on the frozen data. Detector: exactly 200 flags of 50,000.
- **Designed-outcome check (main loop, against the key): G 0/12 flagged (pre-registered ≥6 — MISSED); B4 0/1, B5 0/1 (expected top singletons — MISSED); C 0/12 (zero-flag requirement — MET). The 200 detector flags overlap zero answer-key transactions.**
- **Frozen per the design's own pre-commitment** (§3: "any miss is a frozen, reported deviation, not a retune"). Not retuned; nothing regenerated; the plants stand as designed.
- Diagnosis (recorded from config-level analysis, no key needed): (1) `vendor_amount_z` uses the vendor's *full-year* mean/std, and a sustained drift inflates the baseline it is measured against — Teräskontio's H2 z peaks at +2.13 where a clean-baseline z would be ~+6.5; a drift absorbs itself. (2) The pinned neutral edge cases for sparse vendors make single-invoice vendors invisible — B4/B5's only live signal was `log10_amount` (ranks 6,641 and 9,069). (3) Against heavy-tailed organic amounts (lognormal, cv 0.35–0.65), a +19% shift on a tight-σ vendor is small in population terms. All three go into the writeup/notebook as the case's central honest finding: the generic transaction-grain detector caught none of the designed anomalies and 200 statistically-odd-but-benign rows; the rules caught what rules catch; the reasoning layer carries the rest.
- **Consequences, recorded**: the G exemplum's discovery path shifts from the flagged-cluster route to the free hunt (the briefing's pre-registered "top spend movers" sweep; the memo breadcrumb and the Kuormaraitti mirror remain fully discoverable in the data). B4/B5 move from "flagged benign, stand-down expected" to "unflagged benign, no verdict expected" in the 2×2. Marking still uses the original pre-registered expectations, adjudicated with this context (worksheet + report).
- **Cluster list frozen as mechanically produced**: 39 verdict units (13 rule-side; 24 detector-vendor + SINGLES + RESIDUAL; no month reached the ≥15 threshold, so no December month-cluster emerged — the vendor grouping absorbed the flags first). The design's ≈20–22 was an estimate; the rule is what was pre-registered.
- A clearly-labelled **post-hoc detector iteration** (expanding-baseline z, new-vendor signal) may appear in the notebook as analysis — marked post-hoc, never counted against the pre-registration. Decision deferred to Phase 4; Eva-visible.
